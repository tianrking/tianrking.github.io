For arithmetic only, **planning conversion CNY1 = Rp2,200** gives approximately **¥4,036** for the target or **¥3,139–5,468** for the range. This is not a live or promised November exchange rate. Use actual card/ATM/exchange charges and the rupiah ledger. [BI exchange-rate page](https://www.bi.go.id/id/statistik/informasi-kurs/transaksi-bi/default.aspx)

International flights, Hong Kong → Guangzhou, checked bags, insurance, shopping and extra sunrise/show products are separate. **Whole-trip total = actual ground orders + both international tickets + Hong Kong onward travel + insurance / baggage.** Missing flight prices, arrival time and solo nature quote prevent an honest exact grand total.

|---|---:|---|
| City accommodation | Six-bed model Rp780,000, replaced by actual bookings | Extend the existing Jakarta hotel; compare refundable same-area rooms, not remote bargains |
| Two trains | Rp750,000 target; allowance to Rp1,000,000 | Compare seats, arrival and extra room costs; use daytime fallback if needed |
| Temple transport | Rp500,000 total | Single-temple shared transport + Prambanan bus, not a two-day private package |
| Full nature route | Rp2,500,000 target; Rp2–4 million allowance | Compare at least two quotes with identical inclusions; do not use a two-person rate as solo |
| Airports / city rides | Rp600,000 | Transit and fewer needless short rides; contingency for last-train/check-in risk |

Every extra Rp500,000 nature charge adds that amount to the total, about ¥227 using the planning conversion. Six city private rooms at Rp1,500,000–2,400,000 replace the Rp780,000 beds, adding Rp720,000–1,620,000 and producing **Rp9,600,000–10,500,000**, still not dated quotations. Add actual extra hotel nights for train fallbacks.

Subtract separately budgeted tickets if a product includes them. Add excluded mountain rooms, foreign tickets or Surabaya transfer. The cheap midnight group's real comparison is **actual group price + extra Malang room + excluded foreign fees / guide + onward Surabaya transport**, plus overnight driving and later arrival. Do not replace the planned all-inclusive nature allowance with Rp1,000,000 unless accepting a different route.

Withdraw a modest **Rp500,000–1,000,000** initially rather than the entire trip budget; replenish according to fees and use. Pay major rooms/trains/temples/tours through trusted channels or written orders. Carry enough mountain meal cash. Card purchase costs and stored value are different; ask whether tax, parking, tolls and foreign admission are included. Economise on local walking, public transit, suitable economy seats and complete shared-tour inclusions—not unsafe unguided valleys, unfamiliar motorcycle roads or airport cutoffs.

## Weather, volcanoes and hiking safety {/* #safety */}

November is not guaranteed dry and does not guarantee rain every day. BMKG's **November 2026 monthly rainfall outlook** supplies seasonal context, not safe conditions upstream of the waterfall on 6 November or on a summit on 7 November. Check short-range forecasts and warnings before and during the trip, retaining room to shorten activities for rain, cloud and authority decisions. [Monthly outlook](https://www.bmkg.go.id/iklim/prediksi-hujan-bulanan/curah-hujan-deterministik/3) · [Warnings](https://www.bmkg.go.id/cuaca/peringatan-dini-cuaca)

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Waterfall rain, high water or closure | No valley/crossings; viewpoint only if safely open. Payment does not justify risk |
| Volcanic restriction / exclusion zone | Permitted areas only; omit crater or cancel. A mask is not hazardous-gas protection or a substitute for evacuation |
| Early starts / fatigue | Sleep in Malang on 5 Nov, mountain hotel on 6 Nov and Surabaya on 7 Nov; no overnight Ijen |
| Mountain roads / darkness | Legal experienced transport; leave waterfall around midday, aim for arrival before dark; no navigation shortcuts around closures |
| Working port / crowded streets | Keep phone/bag away from traffic and valuables close; no operations areas |
| Heat, wet terrain and food | Water, sunscreen, rain layer, non-slip shoes and dry clothes; stop when unwell; steep descents are not suitable for everyone |
| Ash / flight disruption | Follow airline and airport notices; one city night reduces road risk, not all ash-related flight risk |

Consult [MAGMA](https://magma.esdm.go.id/), the [park](https://bromotenggersemeru.id/) and [official crisis information](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/crisis-management). Closure, evacuation or dangerous road conditions override the itinerary. Health and mobility affect suitability; obtain medical advice where needed, not an operator's blanket assurance. Check insurance activity/altitude exclusions, medical costs and evacuation; an ordinary urban policy is not assumed to cover mountain activities.

Police **110**, medical **119**. Save insurance, hotel and operator contacts; seek local assistance if roaming cannot connect. [Police](https://polri.go.id/kontak/call-center-110) · [Health ministry](https://keslan.kemkes.go.id/unduhan/fileunduhan_1661490033_323814.pdf) · [Chinese consular information](https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmcg/ljmdd/yz_645708/ydnxy_648376/aqff_648394/)

## Entry and packing {/* #entry */}

For mainland Chinese ordinary-passport tourism, prepare **B1 visa on arrival / e-VoA**, initially up to **30 days**, official fee **Rp500,000**, with at least **six months' passport validity** on entry, SUB → HKG exit ticket and first-night address. VOA eligibility is not visa exemption. [eVisa](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) · [B1 conditions](https://bontang.imigrasi.go.id/layanan-publik/kategori/wna/sub/informasi-visa-republik-indonesia) · [Eligibility lists](https://bontang.imigrasi.go.id/layanan-publik/kategori/wna/sub/daftar-subjek-voa-bvk-calling-visa)

Complete **All Indonesia** inside the official three-day-before-arrival window using the actual landing date and save its QR. It is a **free integrated declaration, not a visa**. No supposed official filing fee to an intermediary. [Official form](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/) · [Free-declaration notice](https://batam.imigrasi.go.id/category/berita-utama/penumpang-yang-tiba-di-tiga-bandara-internasional-dan-pelabuhan-di-batam-wajib-mengisi-deklarasi-kedatangan-melalui-aplikasi-all-indonesia)

Store offline passport, visa, flight, train, temple, hotel, transfer and insurance records. Cover shoulders and wear long trousers / comfortable shoes for religious visits. Add waterfall dry clothes and waterproof bags; mountain layers, headlamp and gloves, not tropical shirts alone. Power banks, liquids and baggage follow airline rules; rail allowances do not establish flight allowances.

## Delay, ticket and weather alternatives {/* #alternatives */}

- **Late 30 Oct arrival:** hotel only; if tired on D2, drop harbour/tower, preserve BI, Glodok and Bahari. Do not move the National Museum to Monday.
- **Unsuitable 1 Nov train:** compare other stations/classes/arrivals; otherwise add 1 Nov hotel and take 2 Nov daytime train. Drop D4 water palace/streets; keep temple dates.
- **No suitable 3 Nov structure session:** try another same-day session. If only 4 Nov works, confirm swapping the temple days and whether the palace fits 3 Nov morning. An afternoon Borobudur visit remains a Borobudur-only day. No Structure means explicitly accepting no climb; Ground is not equivalent.
- **Unsuitable second night train:** search 5 Nov after midnight first; otherwise add 4 Nov Yogyakarta hotel and travel daytime 5 Nov, dropping Malang sightseeing. If even evening arrival fails, rearrange with the nature operator; do not buy a 6 Nov arrival to chase a departed group.
- **Unsafe 6 Nov waterfall:** safe open upper area only or cancel. Wanting both sites is not a reason to force the valley; adjust safe transfers.
- **7 Nov volcano closure:** permitted safe alternatives or earlier Surabaya/museum time; no unknown off-road detour or Ijen night run.
- **Late Surabaya arrival:** omit Tunjungan, sleep; cemetery then museum next day. Retain the night between mountains and flight.
- **Unclear bus / traffic on 8 Nov:** leave earlier or take a ride; Ampel/coffee are removable, check-in is not.
- **More comfort on the same dates:** remove one nature site to recover an early start and long transfer. Keeping both means this compact route, without further remote additions; another day is needed for a slower temple split.
