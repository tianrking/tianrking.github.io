### What night trains save, and the fallback {/* #night-train-fallback */}

**Net saving = actual hotel night avoided − extra train cost versus daytime − additional transfers − early check-in/day-room charges.** A more expensive night train may still buy daylight, but it is not automatically the cheapest option. If the second train's extras exceed a room cost, the daytime train is sensible.
