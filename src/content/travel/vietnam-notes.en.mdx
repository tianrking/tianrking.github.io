## Dongxing and Móng Cái: two sides of the border {/* #heritage */}

Dongxing is in China’s Guangxi region; Móng Cái is across the border in Vietnam’s Quảng Ninh Province. The two towns are often mentioned together, but they are different places. Details such as the first sign in Móng Cái or the first stop after crossing will make this section more useful and more personal once they can be checked against the trip photographs.

## Hạ Long Bay: a body of water, not a departure pier {/* #ha-long */}

An isolated limestone tower gives little sense of the scale of the bay. Viewed together, ridgelines, overlapping islands and passages of water explain why it reads as a landscape rather than an individual sight. The World Heritage listing includes the Cát Bà Archipelago, but the name of the listing does not establish that this trip included a landing on Cát Bà. No cruise, cave or overnight stay is inferred. [UNESCO: Hạ Long Bay–Cát Bà Archipelago](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/672/)

## Đà Nẵng: the central coast and Champa {/* #da-nang */}

Đà Nẵng stands on its own, not just as a transfer for Hội An. The coast and the banks of the Hàn River explain the shape of the modern city. Another layer appears in the Museum of Cham Sculpture, whose sandstone works from different sites connect the city with the history of Champa. The collection is context here, not a claim to have visited the museum. [Vietnam Tourism: Đà Nẵng and the Museum of Cham Sculpture](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/must-do-da-nang-an-insider-list/)

## Ho Chi Minh City: more than one public memory {/* #ho-chi-minh-city */}

The journey finishes in a city where “Saigon” and “Ho Chi Minh City” both appear in public life. A street photograph, a building façade and a museum caption may each give the name a different meaning. If the photographs show central District 1, they can anchor a specific passage about the older streets. If they show the War Remnants Museum or the Independence Palace, those can illuminate two distinct subjects: civilian experience of war and political memory. Neither is counted here as a confirmed visit. [Vietnam Tourism: museum guide](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/best-museums-vietnam/)
